Fiscal sponsorship

hackPHS is fiscally sponsored by The Hack Foundation (d.b.a. Hack Club), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN: 81-2908499).

Donations made through HCB are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law, and donors receive a receipt automatically.

For transparency, HCB deducts a flat 7% fiscal-sponsorship fee from incoming funds.
