01 / About

What is
hackPHS?

hackPHS hosts over 350 high school students from across New Jersey to collaborate and create in a 24-hour, student-organized hackathon at PHS. hackPHS is designed for anyone and for everyone, experienced or beginner.

*Open-source hackathon

hackPHS is built around open source. Hackers are encouraged to publish their code, use open-source tools, and share what they learn. See what hackers built last year.

350+students
24hours together
$0to attend
1weekend to build
Read the hackPHS event guide ↗

Fun fact: We're the largest hackathon hosted by a high school in New Jersey.

You don't need a perfect idea or years of experience. Just bring a laptop, some curiosity, and a willingness to try something new!

04 / FAQs

Before you
pack your bag!

Everything first-time and returning hackers usually want to know. Still wondering about something? Email team@hackphs.tech

01 Who can attend this event?

hackPHS 2026 is for high school students. If you are no longer in high school and would like to help, consider becoming a mentor, chaperone, or volunteer by emailing team@hackphs.tech

02 How do I apply?

Registration has closed. Join the waitlist and we'll reach out if a spot opens. Fill out the waitlist form to request a spot. You can also find hackPHS 2026 on Devpost.

03 How do I become a mentor, chaperone, or volunteer?

Email team@hackphs.tech with how you would like to help, your availability, and any relevant experience.

04 What will be provided during the event?

Free food and drinks will be provided, including options for common dietary restrictions. We will also have designated quiet sleeping areas and electrical outlets throughout the cafeteria for charging devices.

05 What should I bring?

Bring a laptop, charger, any project hardware, a sleeping bag, a change of clothes, toiletries, and anything else you need to stay comfortable overnight.

06 How do I form a team?

Teams of up to 4 people are encouraged (though not mandatory). If you don't have a team before the event, don't worry! At the beginning of the event, there will be a team-building workshop, which will be a great way to meet other enthusiastic people and even make new friends.

07 Is there a Code of Conduct?

All hackers must follow Major League Hacking's Code of Conduct and Princeton High School rules while on campus. All hackers should and are expected to respect one another and help make the event safe and welcoming to participate in.

08 What can I win?

hackPHS hopes to emphasize learning and collaboration more so than competition—nonetheless, there will be awesome prizes to be won for some of the best hacks made during the event! Check out the prizes, including gear, research scholarships, and subscriptions. There are also perks for every participant!

09 What can I learn?

While you can learn a great deal in the process of building an ambitious project in such a limited time frame, we also plan to offer many introductory workshops (around 1 hour each) during the event for those new to software and/or hardware engineering. Last year's topics included Python, Web Development, and Circuitry, among many others—check our schedule for the full list!

10 Where can I find hackPHS on social media?

Follow @hackphs on Instagram for announcements and behind-the-scenes updates.

11 My question is not answered here.

Send it to team@hackphs.tech and the team will help.
